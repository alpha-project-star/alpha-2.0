# New Organic Orb Design Concepts

I've moved away from the mechanical, "sticker" aesthetic to embrace something more organic, fluid, and alive.

### Concept 1: The Bioluminescent Pulse
This concept focuses on a soft, glowing, translucent membrane with fluid, inner motion. It feels like a living cell or a concentrated drop of ethereal energy. It's minimalist and focuses purely on the organic, breathing light.

![Bioluminescent Pulse Orb](/src/assets/images/bioluminescent_pulse_orb_1792648043440.jpg)

### Concept 2: The Fluid Nebula Eye
This concept is more complex, suggesting a deeper, shifting "neural core" with nebula-like fluidity, representing active processing. It keeps the core "orb" feel while making it feel like a living, shifting consciousness rather than a mechanical lens.

![Fluid Nebula Eye Orb](/src/assets/images/fluid_nebula_eye_1792648055339.jpg)

These designs are meant to be animated in code (using SVG filters and CSS animations) to capture that "alive" feeling. Do either of these feel closer to what you're looking for?
